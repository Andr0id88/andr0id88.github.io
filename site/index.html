Scope & integrations

Slack/vitibot requests connect to cluster creation, changes, deletion, access, and ROR project workflows. Kubernetes and Talos inventory feeds version checks and upgrade readiness. Argo CD and ROR provide application state, metadata, and access context.

Hub & agents

A Go hub and distributed agents collect information across sites and clusters. Operational workflows include permission checks, plan review, and audit records. Missing or stale reports stay visible rather than being treated as healthy.

Resilience engineering

Viti Center highlights workloads that could lose service after a single worker-node failure. Placement and anti-affinity findings give teams specific workloads to investigate, alongside KubeVirt capacity, Ceph health, operator diagnostics, and networking information.

Operational value

An operator can follow a request into provisioning, investigate the resulting cluster and its tooling, and review migration progress in one workspace. I develop this tooling alongside my contribution to the Tanzu-to-Vitistack transition.

The problem I wanted to solve

Working across AI coding agents means keeping track of sessions, ongoing tasks, activity, and usage. I wanted a Linux workspace organised around how I actually work.

What I built

Hivemind brings coding-agent sessions, activity, and usage visibility into a Linux desktop and terminal workspace. The Brain graph connects shared notes, role teams organise agents around a task, and reusable prompts make recurring work easier to start. Contextual actions let me request a review or second opinion from another agent.
